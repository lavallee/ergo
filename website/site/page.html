Not defects — calls somebody made about what may be computed from this data, and what the naive move would have been. Reached by the question you are answering, not by what is broken.

What this page costs to keep

Five issues and two practices, in one file, alongside the code that ingests the data. The bulk of it was written at the moment of discovery — the workaround commit and the issue entry are the same change — which is the only authoring discipline in the spec that really matters. An issue discovered but not registered is the next person's rediscovery, and the next reporter's error.
